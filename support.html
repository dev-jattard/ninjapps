Common questions

The app is not showing my coordinates or address

Tap Refresh to request a location fix. If nothing appears, check that location access is allowed for the app:

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services. Make sure Location Services is on, then find the app in the list and choose While Using the App.
  • Android: Settings › Apps › (app name) › Permissions › Location. Choose Allow only while using the app.

A first fix can take up to a minute outdoors, and GPS is weak or unavailable indoors, underground, and in parking garages.

My coordinates look slightly wrong

Accuracy comes from the device's own GPS and network positioning, not from us. Being outdoors with a clear view of the sky, and having Wi-Fi turned on, both improve it.

The address is blank but the coordinates are there

Addresses come from a reverse geocoding lookup, which needs a working internet connection and does not cover every location. The coordinates are still valid and can be copied or shared on their own.

How do I remove the ads?

Use the Remove Ads option inside the free app, or install the paid ad-free edition. Removing ads is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I already paid to remove ads but I am seeing them again

This usually happens after reinstalling or changing device. Open the Remove Ads option and tap Restore. That re-checks your store account and restores the purchase at no further cost. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you originally bought it with. If it still does not restore, email us with your store receipt and we will sort it out.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store rather than by us. On iOS, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use your Google Play order history. We are happy to help if you get stuck.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email studio@ninjapps.com. Steps to reproduce a bug, plus a screenshot, make a real difference. Feature requests genuinely do shape what we build next.

What data do the apps collect?

See our Privacy Policy. Your location is used to produce the coordinates and address shown on screen. The free editions show ads, and you can change your advertising consent choices at any time from within the app.
