Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 17 August 2026

This policy explains what our apps do with your information, in plain terms. The short version: we run no servers of our own, we hold no personal data about you, and your coordinates are never sent to us.

1. Who we are

These apps are made by Jason Attard, trading as NinjApps ("we", "us", "our"). We are the data controller for the limited processing described below.

You can reach us at studio@ninjapps.com. We answer privacy questions ourselves; there is no separate team to route you through.

2. Which apps this covers

  • Find Me: GPS Coordinates, and Find Me: GPS Coordinates + (the ad-free edition)
  • Find My Car: Where I Parked, and Find My Car: Where I Parked + (the ad-free edition)

Where something applies only to the free editions, which show ads, we say so.

3. Your location, and where it goes

This matters most, so it comes first.

When you ask for a location fix, the app reads your device's position using the operating system's location services. That position is shown on your screen and, in Find My Car, saved on your device so you can return to it.

We never receive your coordinates. We have no server for them to be sent to. Your position leaves your device in only three situations:

  • To turn coordinates into an address. The app asks your operating system's geocoding service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android) to look up the address for your coordinates. That lookup is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.
  • When you choose to send it. Using Copy, Share, or Map hands the coordinates to whatever app or person you pick. That is your action, and where it goes from there is outside our control.
  • Advertising, in the free editions only, and only if you consent. See section 4.

You can refuse or withdraw location access at any time in your device settings. The apps remain usable with location switched off; they simply cannot show a position until you allow it and ask for one.

4. Advertising (free editions only)

The free editions are funded by ads served through Google AdMob. The ad-free editions contain no advertising code and this section does not apply to them.

To show ads, Google may store and read information on your device, including advertising identifiers, and may use it to select and measure ads. Depending on the consent you give, Google and its advertising partners may also use device information and location data for personalised advertising.

How your choice is collected. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before requesting any ad. No ad is requested until you have answered it. Outside those regions, ads are served without a consent form where local law permits.

How to change your mind. You can reopen the consent options from within the app at any time and withdraw or change your consent. Withdrawing consent does not remove ads; it limits how your data may be used to select them. Your device settings also let you reset your advertising identifier or ask apps not to use it.

Tracking. Our apps do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and do not request the advertising identifier on iOS. On iOS, ad measurement uses Apple's own privacy-preserving attribution instead.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies: how Google uses data in AdMob and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the apps are used in aggregate, for example how many people who open the app reach a location fix.

We deliberately log almost nothing. The events we record carry only short, fixed values from a closed list, such as whether a location permission was granted or refused, that coordinates were shown, which button was pressed, and how a purchase ended. There is no field anywhere in the app that accepts free text.

We never log your latitude, longitude, address, place name, or a device identifier. This is enforced in the code itself, not left to discretion.

Firebase also collects some information automatically, such as app opens, device model, operating system version, country, and a randomly generated app instance identifier. That collection is Google's, described in Google Analytics for Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Purchases

The free editions offer a one-time purchase to remove ads, and the + editions are paid apps.

We do not process payments and never see your payment details. All purchases are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play, under their terms and privacy policies. The app asks the store whether this account owns the purchase, and receives back only that answer. We receive no name, no email address, and no card information.

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us. See our support page for how to request one.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we never have.
  • We operate no servers or databases, so there is no store of user data for us to search, leak, or hand over.
  • We do not combine data across our apps to build a profile of you.

8. Legal bases for processing

Where the UK GDPR or the EU GDPR applies, we rely on the following:

  • Providing the app's core function (reading your position, showing it, saving a parking location on your device): necessary to deliver the service you asked for.
  • Personalised advertising and storing information on your device for advertising, in the free editions: your consent, collected through the form described in section 4, and withdrawable at any time.
  • Analytics: our legitimate interest in understanding how the apps are used well enough to improve them, balanced against the fact that the data is aggregate and contains no location or contact information.
  • Purchases and restoring them: necessary to perform the transaction you entered into.

9. Retention

Anything the app saves for you, such as a parking location or your preferences, stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

We hold no personal data ourselves, so we have nothing to retain or delete on our side. Data held by Google for advertising and analytics is retained according to Google's own retention periods, described in the policies linked above.

10. International transfers

Google and Apple are global companies and may process the data described above outside your country, including in the United States. Those transfers are made under the safeguards those companies put in place, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Because we send nothing ourselves, we make no international transfers of our own.

11. Your rights

If the EU or UK GDPR applies to you, you have the right to request access to your personal data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

In practice, the fastest route for most of these is your own device and the companies that hold the data: your device settings control location access and your advertising identifier, the in-app consent options control advertising consent, and deleting the app removes everything stored locally.

For anything you want from us, write to studio@ninjapps.com. We will respond within one month. Be aware that because we hold no identifiable data about you, we usually cannot locate "your" records, and will explain that rather than guess.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled a request, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In Malta, where we are based, that is the Office of the Information and Data Protection Commissioner.

12. Children

Our apps are general-purpose utilities and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. In the European Economic Area, consent for advertising in the free editions should be given by someone aged 16 or over, or by a parent or guardian, in line with the age set by local law. If you believe a child has used the apps in a way that needs our attention, contact us and we will act on it.

13. Security

Because your location and preferences stay on your device, their security depends largely on your device: a passcode, up-to-date software, and not jailbreaking or rooting it all help. For the limited data handled by Apple and Google on our behalf, we rely on their security measures. No app or service can promise absolute security, and we do not.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the apps change in a way that affects it, and we will move the effective date at the top. Significant changes to how we use your data will be flagged in the app where we can. Continuing to use the apps after a change means you accept the updated policy.

15. Contact

Questions, requests, or corrections: studio@ninjapps.com. A real person reads it.
